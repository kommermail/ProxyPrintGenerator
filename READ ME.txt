ProxyPrintGenerator for Windows
================================

Print playtest proxies of your decks: paste a deck list, pick printings,
and get print-ready Letter sheets (3x3 cards at true size, with cutting
guides), as PDF, PNG or JPEG, or print straight from the app.

RUNNING IT
- Unzip anywhere and double click ProxyPrintGenerator.exe.
  Nothing to install; Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit.
- The first time, Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC"
  because the app isn't code signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

WHERE THINGS GO
- Finished builds:  Documents\ProxyPrintGenerator\
- Your decks:       %APPDATA%\ProxyPrintGenerator\decks.json
- Image caches:     %LOCALAPPDATA%\ProxyPrintGenerator\  (safe to delete)

PRINTING TIPS
- Print at actual size. The app's own Print button always does this.
- Most home inkjets print at 600 dpi, so a 600 dpi build ("600") is all
  they can use; higher scales are for print shops.
- If the bottom row gets clipped, turn on Safe area or Shift up.
